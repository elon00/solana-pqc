# SCSTOBCMinority AI — Core Purpose

SCSTOBCMinority AI is a Solana-based research project whose long-term mission is to support the dignity, empowerment, opportunity and upliftment of SC, ST, OBC, minority and other underserved communities through transparent, rights-respecting digital infrastructure.

## Guiding Values

**Love · Peace · Joy · Harmony · Truth · Charity · Unity · Equality · Dignity · Liberty · Consent · Knowledge · Service · Justice**

## Human-Development Goals

The ecosystem may support lawful, accountable programs related to:

- food, nutrition and basic necessities;
- clothing and shelter;
- housing support;
- healthcare;
- education, scholarships and skills;
- jobs, livelihoods and entrepreneurship;
- financial literacy, savings and responsible wealth-building;
- lawful land and productive-asset access;
- scientific learning, technology, tools, machines and productive materials;
- charitable gifts and community grants;
- agriculture, fruits and nutrition;
- dignity, anti-discrimination and access to rights;
- peaceful civic awareness and access to public services;
- freedom of thought, conscience, religion or belief;
- voluntary adult family wellbeing and consensual relationship support;
- philosophy, ethics, spirituality and freedom of inquiry.

The phrase “men-machine-material powers” is interpreted as building **human capability, technological capability and productive resources**, never control or ownership over other people.

The aspiration to understand the “secrets of creation or the Creator” is treated as a philosophical, scientific and spiritual quest for knowledge, not a technical or supernatural promise.

## What SPQC Is For

SPQC is intended as an ecosystem utility token for future community programs such as transparent grants, scholarships, training incentives, health and nutrition support, livelihood programs, technology access, research grants, open-source development and accountable community treasuries.

The current implementation uses an **uncapped application-level mint policy**. “Uncapped” does not mean mathematically infinite; SPL Token supply accounting is ultimately bounded by Solana's raw `u64` supply representation.

## Non-Negotiable Safeguards

SPQC must never be used to:

- buy or condition public votes;
- reward political allegiance;
- require religious conversion;
- assign, buy, sell or coerce a spouse or relationship;
- deny equal rights based on token ownership;
- claim guaranteed profit, wealth, jobs, housing, land, health outcomes, precious metals or any other personal result;
- place sensitive beneficiary data openly on-chain.

Participation should be voluntary, lawful, non-coercive and compatible with equal human dignity.

## Project Standard

The project should be judged by:

1. verifiable technology;
2. transparent treasury rules;
3. measurable community benefit;
4. privacy and safety;
5. independent security and financial review;
6. truthful separation of implemented, prototype and planned features;
7. equal dignity and human rights for every participant.
